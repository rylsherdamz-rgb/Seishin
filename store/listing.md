## App name (max 30)
Seishin: Calendar & Tasks

## Short description (max 80)
Your calendar, tasks, notes and alarms in one calm app. Works offline.

## Full description (max 4000)
Seishin keeps your day in one place: your schedule, your to-dos and your notes, with reminders that actually get your attention.

Everything lives on your phone. No account and no sign-up, and it works fully offline.

📅 CALENDAR THAT READS AT A GLANCE
• Timeline view shows your day hour by hour, with a live "now" line
• Agenda view lists what's next; Upcoming shows the days ahead
• A week strip with dots shows which days are busy
• Today's tasks sit right above your schedule

✅ TASKS WITHOUT THE CLUTTER
• Add a task in one line, then tap Today, Tomorrow or Next week
• Grouped automatically: Overdue, Today, Upcoming, No date
• Checklists inside a task, with progress like "2/4 done"
• Persistent reminders for things you really can't miss

📝 NOTES
• Quick notes with colors and pins
• Keep lists, formulas, ideas and meeting notes next to your plans

⏰ ALARMS THAT RING
• Event reminders ring as a full-screen alarm, even on the lock screen
• Snooze or dismiss right from the alarm
• Choose your own ringtone
• Alarms come back by themselves after your phone restarts

🔗 BRING YOUR LIFE IN
• Sync with your phone calendars (Google, Outlook, Samsung)
• Subscribe to school calendar links (Canvas, Classroom, Moodle, any .ics)
• Connect Todoist and Notion
• Scan a photo or screenshot of an assignment or schedule, then review it and save it as a task or event

🤖 AN ASSISTANT THAT CAN ACT
• Ask in plain words: "move my dentist appointment to Friday", "what's due this week?"
• It can create and update events, tasks and notes for you
• Runs on-device, or on a cloud model if you add your own key

🎨 MAKE IT YOURS
• Light and dark themes, six accent colors, adjustable text size
• Home screen widgets: Next up, Agenda, Month, Tasks and Quick add

🔒 YOUR DATA STAYS YOURS
• Stored locally on your device, with no Seishin account or server
• Back up to a file and restore whenever you want
• Data only leaves your phone through connections you turn on yourself: calendar sync, Todoist, Notion, or a cloud AI key

Seishin is built for students and busy people who want one calm place for their day, not five apps.
