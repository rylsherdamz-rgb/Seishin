## Session: calendar sync, reminders, widgets
- Root cause of "calendar sync is bad": events were placed on their UTC day (`startDate.split("T")[0]`). In UTC+8 everything before 08:00 and every all-day event showed a day early. Now keyed by local day. Android all-day events (stored at UTC midnight) are converted both ways, multi-day all-day events span their days, cancelled instances are skipped.
- Root causes of "no notification when due": synced Google events never had reminders; launch only scheduled *today's* events; task-reminder channel was only created at launch (Android drops posts to a missing channel); notification action/tap listeners were never registered unless notification-listener access was granted; no exact-alarm permission (Android 14 delays inexact alarms in Doze). Old code also re-rang months-old events on edit.
- New: rolling event-alarm planner (event-alarm-plan.ts, tested) run on launch, after every sync and in the background; Google events keep their own reminder or get a default (10 min, configurable); Settings → Reminders has a test notification + battery shortcut.
- Sync now also runs on every return to the app (phone calendars only, cheap) and on pull-to-refresh in the calendar.
- Widgets (Android, react-native-android-widget): Today, Tasks (tick off in place), Month (paging), Next up, Quick add. Themed from the app theme; deep links via app/open.tsx; "Add" buttons in Settings pin them.

## Reflection
